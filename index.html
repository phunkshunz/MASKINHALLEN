Built in a shipyard.

In the 90s, Stockholm's heaviest techno club was in a rattling shipyard. It was called Docklands.

That's where I learned how techno should feel: the bass that makes the steel plates rattle, the floor that sweats, and the rhythm that drags behind, stumbles, and still always lands on the one.

Someone once said my music sounds like a damp concrete floor. I took it as a compliment.

BETONG, ARMERING and FUKT are built in Maskinhallen to sound like that floor.

FAQ

Good to know.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Intel or Apple Silicon. The plugins come as VST3 and AU and work in Bitwig, Ableton Live, Logic and other DAWs that load VST3 or AU.

How do I install?

After buying you get a zip file. Unzip it and run the install script. It takes less than a minute. Then find the plugin in your DAW.

Does the demo sound like the plugin?

The demo is a sketch running in your browser, with five presets and a few knobs. The plugins have more tracks, more knobs, better filters and oversampled distortion, so they sound heavier.

Do I get updates?

Yes. New versions of what you bought are free.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. macOS only for now.

How do I pay?

By card or PayPal at checkout. VAT is calculated automatically based on where you live.
