About

Built in a
shipyard.

In the 90s, Stockholm's heaviest techno club was in a rattling shipyard. It was called Docklands.

That's where I learned how techno should feel: the bass that makes the steel plates rattle, the floor that sweats, and the rhythm that drags behind, stumbles, and still always lands on the one.

Someone once said my music sounds like a damp concrete floor. I took it as a compliment.

I have spent twenty years working with my hands on machines. Now I build the ones I always wanted in my studio.

Phunkshunz AUDIO is a name I have carried since I was a kid. It is a play on "function": machines that do one thing, and do it hard.

FAQ

Good to know.

Anything else? Ask through the store and you get an answer from the person who built the machines.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 11 Big Sur or later (Intel or Apple Silicon). BETONG and ARMERING also run on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. They work in Bitwig, Ableton Live, Logic, FL Studio, Reaper and other hosts that load VST3 or AU.

How do I install?

After buying you get a zip file. Unzip it and run the install script: installera.command on Mac, installera.bat on Windows. It takes less than a minute. Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

Do I get updates?

Yes. New versions of what you bought are free.

Is there a Windows version?

BETONG and ARMERING come with both the Mac and the Windows version. The other machines are macOS for now. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway.

How do I pay?

By card or PayPal at checkout through Payhip. VAT is calculated automatically based on where you live. You get the download link straight away.
