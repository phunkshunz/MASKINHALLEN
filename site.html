Built in a shipyard.

FAQ

Good to know.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 11 Big Sur or later (Intel or Apple Silicon), or a PC with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. On Mac you get VST3 and AU, on Windows VST3. They work in Bitwig, Ableton Live, Logic, FL Studio, Reaper and other DAWs that load VST3 or AU.

How do I install?

After buying you get a zip file for Mac and one for Windows. Unzip the one for your computer and run the install script: installera.command on Mac, installera.bat on Windows. It takes less than a minute. Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

Does the demo sound like the plugin?

The demo is a sketch running in your browser, with five presets and a few knobs. The plugins have more tracks, more knobs, better filters and oversampled distortion, so they sound heavier.

Do I get updates?

Yes. New versions of what you bought are free.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. Every purchase includes both the Mac and the Windows version, so you can use them on both. On Windows you also get a standalone app that runs without a DAW. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway.

How do I pay?

By card or PayPal at checkout. VAT is calculated automatically based on where you live.
